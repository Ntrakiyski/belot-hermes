# Product

## Register

product

## Users
Bulgarian Belot players opening the game on their phones, and Rado validating rules and requesting improvements through Telegram. They need to follow each turn without reading technical logs.

## Product Purpose
A server-authoritative Bulgarian Belot game with understandable bidding, legal moves, visible tricks, declarations and explained scores. The current DEMO offers guest friend tables and clearly separate practice; authenticated matchmaking remains a later milestone.

## Brand Personality
Familiar, calm, clear. Bulgarian game language, concise feedback and a recognisable card table.

## Anti-references
The previous screen's disappearing tricks, debug-first presentation, uneven wrapped hand and silent connection failures. Do not imitate Croatian rules or copy the reference repository's code/assets.

## Design Principles
- Show each meaningful action and the winning trick before advancing.
- Keep partners opposite and turn direction consistent with Bulgarian play.
- Present only legal actions and explain scoring in the game itself.
- Make practice explicit; never represent bots as human multiplayer.
- Preserve the existing felt-table identity and prioritise usable cards over decoration.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Readable high-contrast rank and suit labels, named controls, keyboard focus, disabled illegal moves, reduced-motion support and a phone layout without horizontal page overflow. Never rely on suit colour alone.
