# Product judgment
1. Identify who's helped and what measurable result improves (completion, trust, latency, invite success).
2. Compare simplest fix, intermediate solution and costly ideal. Recommend the cheapest reversible option that solves the underlying need.
3. Protect P0: playable four-human Belot, correct rules, social entry points, reliability, authorized diagnostics.
4. Keep cosmetics/rankings/payment/novel technologies deferred unless required by core validation or explicitly prioritized.
5. Give Rado one paragraph with your choice and a short "later" note; don't request sign-off on trivial implementation details.
