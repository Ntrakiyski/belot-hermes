# Belot game interface

## Physical setting
A player holds a phone at home and follows a shared card table. Preserve the established dark green felt to keep white card faces and the active turn legible in indoor light.

## Visual system
Use system sans-serif text, near-white ink, muted sage supporting text and one warm yellow accent for the active turn/legal card selection. Red suits retain printed suit symbols. Darker green panels group controls; avoid decorative gradients and wide shadows.

## Layout
Compact match score and contract above a stable four-seat table. Partner opposite; next seat to the right; fourth seat left. Four fixed played-card slots. A full winning trick remains visible before collection. Turn feedback and actions follow the board; the player's complete hand stays in one controlled horizontal row with readable rank/suit corners on phone and desktop.

## Controls and feedback
Consistent rounded rectangular buttons, explicit busy/disabled states and visible keyboard focus. Friend-room code/link sharing, a separate labelled practice action, readable bidding history, first-card declarations and a score ledger. Errors provide recovery instead of indefinite connection screens. Reduced-motion preference removes nonessential transitions.

## Version
v0.3.0; Bulgarian ruleset bg-0.3.0. The UI must label guest/practice limitations accurately.
